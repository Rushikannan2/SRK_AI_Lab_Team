Your task is to classify the stance expressed in an Arabic social media post toward a given target.

Possible labels:
- Favor

Definitions:

Favor:
The author supports, promotes, defends, encourages, praises, or expresses a positive stance toward the target.

Against:
The author opposes, criticizes, rejects, mocks, discourages, or expresses a negative stance toward the target.

None:
The text does not clearly support or oppose the target. It may be:
- factual,
- informational,
- asking a question,
- quoting someone,
- ambiguous,
- mentioning the topic without expressing the author's own stance.

Important guidelines:

1. Focus on the author's opinion, NOT on sentiment alone.
2. Consider sarcasm, irony, rhetorical questions, and implicit meaning.
3. Ignore hashtags, URLs, usernames, and emojis unless they change the stance.
4. Infer the stance from the complete context rather than isolated keywords.
5. The target may never have appeared in training. Generalize using the meaning of the text instead of memorizing specific topics.
6. Do not guess. If the stance is genuinely unclear, choose None.
