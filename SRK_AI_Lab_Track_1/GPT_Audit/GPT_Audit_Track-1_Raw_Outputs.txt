### 6. Where Qwen is stronger

Qwen remains useful as an independent check, especially when:

* the anti-driving hashtag is accompanied by explicit negative content,
* the stance is direct,
* or the tweet is a conventional argument rather than a highly sarcastic joke.

However, Qwen appears slightly more prone than Gemini to treating **contextual criticism as directional stance**.

### 8. Are the audited labels reliable enough?

**Yes, overall.**

The first 200 examples now show a very consistent pattern:

* Explicit statements → high confidence.
* Strong sarcasm → usually resolvable.
* Hashtag/content contradiction → must read the tweet, not the hashtag.
* Factual/procedural statements → often **None**.
* Topic criticism ≠ necessarily stance against the target.
* Supporting women generally ≠ necessarily supporting driving unless the target connection is clear.

The fact that Gemini is strongest **while still having identifiable failure modes** is actually much more convincing than simply observing that Gemini wins by majority vote.
